# Connected AI client icons

Reused from EverBond's bundled `ios/EverBondApp/Resources/Assets.xcassets/AIClient-*.imageset/icon.png` on 2026-11-24, preserving the original artwork. ChatGPT, DeepSeek and Z.ai use their alpha masks for adaptive monochrome rendering. Other icons retain their original colors. Qwen Work and Doubao Work are matched before the consumer apps; optical scales follow EverBond.

These marks identify the connected client by its registered display name. Fuzzy matching is cosmetic, not an authenticity or trust signal. Unknown names use the link symbol. All assets ship locally; no remote image requests.

See EverBond `public/ai/icons/SOURCES.md` for upstream provenance: Gemini and Z.ai from the vendored LobeHub icon set; DeepSeek from its official favicon; Qwen and Doubao from their product artwork. Product names and marks remain owned by their respective owners.
