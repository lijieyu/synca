# Synca 1.2.0 (89) — Microsoft Store first release
Status: Saved in Partner Center submission draft; not submitted for certification. Publisher: Jieyu Li. Store ID: 9PJ7VZ48QM81.
Category: Productivity
Short description: Capture ideas, tasks and useful information, then pick up on your other devices.

Synca helps you quickly capture thoughts, tasks and everyday information, and sync them across your own devices.

Record text and images, organize information with categories, use Markdown to highlight what matters, mark tasks complete, and find content with search. Sign in to the same Synca account to continue on Windows, Mac, iPhone, iPad and the Web.

Free accounts support text and image records within the account's storage allowance. General file uploads and MCP access require the corresponding Pro entitlement. Account entitlements follow you across devices. Cross-device sync requires an account and an internet connection.

Microsoft Store manages installation and updates for this edition. Sign in when you first open the Store edition to access your cloud content. Your website edition's local files are not deleted.
