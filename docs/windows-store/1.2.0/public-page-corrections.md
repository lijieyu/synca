## Privacy — 简体中文

简介：说明 Synca 如何收集、使用、存储和保护 Web、iOS、Mac 和 Windows 间同步所涉及的用户数据。

在“我们收集的信息”中保留现有 Apple 账户说明，并增加：使用邮箱登录时提供的邮箱地址，以及验证登录请求所需的信息。

## Privacy — English

Description: How Synca collects, uses, stores, and protects data across Web, iOS, Mac, and Windows.

Keep the existing Apple account disclosure and add: Your email address when you use email sign-in, and information needed to verify your sign-in request.

User content: Content you choose to create or upload, including text entries, images and files, and metadata such as creation time and source device name.

Storage: Account information and records are stored on Synca's backend infrastructure. Uploaded images and files are stored on the server so you can access them on your other devices.

产品范围首段：Synca 用于个人快速记录和跨设备同步。你可以使用应用提供的 Apple 或邮箱登录方式访问账户，并在自己的设备之间同步文字、图片和文件；普通文件上传需要相应 Pro 权益。

## Support — English

Platform: Windows, Mac, iPhone, iPad, or Web.

Product scope first paragraph: Synca helps you quickly capture information and sync it across your own devices. Use the Apple or email sign-in options offered in the app to access your account and sync text, images and files. Ordinary file uploads require the applicable Pro entitlement.
